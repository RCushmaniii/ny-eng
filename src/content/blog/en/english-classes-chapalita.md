## What "Online" Actually Means for Your English

Some people hear "online class" and picture a recorded video they'll never finish. That's not this. My coaching is **live and one-on-one** — a real conversation, in real time, focused entirely on how you speak, think, and land your point in English.

Being online is an advantage, not a compromise:

- **You practice in your real environment.** You'll be speaking English from the same desk where you take your actual work calls — so the confidence transfers directly.
- **We can share a screen instantly.** Reviewing a real email you need to send, rehearsing slides before a board meeting, marking up phrasing together — all of it happens naturally.
- **No time is wasted.** The hour you'd spend in the car becomes another hour of speaking practice, or an hour back with your family.

For Chapalita professionals who value their time and their calm, that trade is an easy one.

I don't teach English the way a school does. I coach the English a working professional needs to sound clear, credible, and in command. That usually means:
