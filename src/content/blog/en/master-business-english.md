If you already use English at work but feel your delivery doesn't match how good you are at your actual job, **business English coaching** is exactly what you need. This isn't grammar class or a generic course — it's one-on-one, online training focused on the situations that truly define your career, like leading a meeting, presenting to a US client, or negotiating under pressure. I'm Robert Cushman, a New York English teacher, and I help professionals across Mexico close the gap between "I understand everything" and "I can say exactly what I mean, right now, without freezing."

## What Business English Coaching Is

Business English coaching trains how you use the language in your real job, not how you study it in a classroom. That difference matters. Most of the professionals who come to me have spent years reading documentation, writing emails, and following meetings in English. Their problem isn't knowledge — it's real-time performance, when a room full of people (sometimes joining from the United States) is waiting for your answer and there's no time to translate in your head.

That's where coaching separates from any traditional class. Instead of reviewing rules, we train the high-pressure moments until they feel natural: how to take control of a conversation, how to disagree without sounding rude, how to explain a problem without losing the room. You bring the real situations from your work, and we turn them into the practice material.

## How It Differs From a Traditional English Class

An English class teaches you the language. Coaching trains you to perform with it. It's a simple but decisive distinction:

- A class is measured by what you know; coaching, by how you perform when it counts.
- A class uses a textbook; coaching uses your own meetings, emails, and presentations.
- A class gives you theory; coaching gives you honest, actionable feedback every session, so you improve fast.

If you've taken English courses that were "fine" but didn't change how you feel in an important meeting, this is why: you were missing performance training, not more grammar.

## Who This Coaching Is For

I work with professionals who already carry responsibility and for whom English became the language of their next promotion: engineers and tech leads, managers and directors, startup founders, and specialists in finance, sales, operations, and HR who sit in on international meetings. If your work touches a US client or a remote team, English has stopped being a line on your résumé and become a multiplier on everything else you already know how to do.

## What the Coaching Focuses On

The coaching is built around the moments that move your career. These are the areas I train most, each with a dedicated program if you want to go deeper:

- **Executive English and presence** — the phrasing, tone, and register that make you sound confident and authoritative, not just correct. It's the heart of [executive English](/en/services/executive-english/).
- **Presentations** — structuring a clear argument, handling live questions, and sounding convincing instead of rehearsed. We work on it in depth in [public speaking English](/en/services/public-speaking-english/).
- **Meetings and client calls** — steering the conversation, interrupting politely, and [leading meetings with authority](/en/blog/lead-meeting-english-phrases/) instead of just surviving them.
- **Negotiation** — reading tone, softening or sharpening your language on purpose, and [negotiating with confidence](/en/blog/how-to-negotiate-in-english-framework/) without giving up ground for lack of words.
- **Interviews** — preparing for roles at US and international companies with [interview prep](/en/services/interview-prep/), where the interview itself is a live test of the English you'll use on the job.
- **Email and written communication** — sounding senior in writing and avoiding the [email phrases that make you sound junior](/en/blog/email-phrases-that-make-you-sound-junior/).

You can see every program on the [coaching services](/en/services/) page, or, if you'd rather start on your own, explore the library of [free courses](/en/courses/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is business English coaching?

It is one-on-one training focused on how you use English in your real job, not on textbook grammar. Instead of drilling verb tenses, we train the situations that define your career: leading a meeting, presenting to a US client, negotiating a deadline, or answering hard questions under pressure. The goal isn't to know more English — it's to sound as capable in English as you already are at your job.

### How is business English coaching different from a traditional English class?

A traditional class teaches you the language; coaching trains you to perform with it. A class is measured by what you know (vocabulary, grammar, exercises); coaching is measured by how you perform in high-pressure situations, with honest feedback every session and your own meetings, emails, and presentations as the practice material.

### Is business English coaching online or in person?

It's fully online, and that's a feature, not a compromise. Your business English happens on video calls, shared screens, and chat, so we train in the same medium where you actually have to perform. You join from your desk or from home, with no commute and no traffic, and the schedule bends to your work.

### Do I need a minimum level of English to start?

You don't need a specific level or a placement test. Coaching is built for professionals who already function in English but want to sound more confident, clear, and authoritative. We start with a conversation to hear where you actually are and build the plan from there.

### Does coaching work for teams and companies, or only individuals?

Both. I offer individual coaching and training for entire teams and departments through the corporate package. Many companies start with their leaders and then extend the program to the rest of the team.

### How much does business English coaching cost?

The first session is free and no-obligation: we talk through what your work demands and I show you exactly how the coaching would target it. From there, the program is built around your goals and availability, whether that's ongoing one-on-one coaching or a package for your team.
