## Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

**Last Updated:** July 1, 2026

I keep a limited, personally-coached schedule, and every session is reserved specifically for you. This policy exists so that your time — and mine — is respected, and so the terms are clear before you book.

## Cancellation & No-Show Policy

Cancellations and reschedules require at least one business day (24 hours') notice. If you cancel or reschedule with less than 24 hours' notice, the full session fee applies — the same as if you attended. No-shows are treated the same: Robert waits up to 15 minutes, after which it counts as a missed session and the full fee applies.

## Notice Window

Cancellations and reschedules require **at least 24 hours' notice**, which I treat as **one business day**. This gives me enough time to offer the slot to another client and to adjust my schedule.

To count as valid notice, your message must reach me at least 24 hours before your session's scheduled start time.

## Late Cancellations & Reschedules

If you cancel or reschedule with **less than 24 hours' notice**, the **full session fee applies**. Because the time was reserved exclusively for you and can no longer be offered to someone else, a late cancellation is charged the same as an attended session.

This applies equally to cancellations and to last-minute reschedule requests — moving a session inside the 24-hour window is treated the same as a late cancellation.

## No-Shows & Late Arrivals

If you don't arrive for a scheduled session, I wait up to **15 minutes** after the start time. After that, the session is treated as a **missed session** and the **full fee applies**.

If you join late but within those 15 minutes, we'll use whatever time remains — the session still ends at its originally scheduled time.

## Payment

- **Individuals:** Payment is due before each session.
- **Companies:** Corporate clients are invoiced (with a *factura*) monthly at the end of each month for all sessions completed.

## Emergencies

Life happens. If a genuine emergency prevents you from attending, reach out as soon as you're able — I review these situations individually and in good faith. Repeated last-minute cancellations, however, will be charged under the standard policy above.
